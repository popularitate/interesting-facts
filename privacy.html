Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Interesting Facts" service (the "Service") handles personal data. The Service is an automated publishing tool that creates and posts short educational videos to social media accounts owned by the Service operator.

1. Data We Collect

The Service does not offer user accounts and does not collect personal data from members of the public. The only data processed are:

Platform authorization tokens — OAuth tokens for the operator's own social media accounts (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), used solely to publish content to those accounts. Basic account information — such as the account display name returned by the platform during authorization, used only to confirm the correct account is connected.

2. How Data Is Used

Authorization tokens are used exclusively to upload and publish video content to the operator's own accounts through official platform APIs (such as the TikTok Content Posting API). No data is used for advertising, profiling or analytics of third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Tokens are stored on a private, access-controlled server operated by the Service owner, protected by industry-standard measures. They are retained only for as long as the integration is active and are deleted when access is revoked.

4. Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the respective social media platform's official APIs as required to publish content.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Published content appears on third-party platforms governed by their own privacy policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

As the Service processes no public user data, data subject requests are generally not applicable. Platform account holders can revoke the Service's access at any time from their platform's security settings, which invalidates the stored tokens.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
